# 💊 MAATRA — Vernacular Medicine & Visual Dose Companion

**Team Name:** 404 Boys Not Found
**Problem Statement:** PS 2 — Vernacular Medicine & Dose Companion

## 📌 About the Project

MAATRA is a healthcare assistance platform designed to make medicine information easier to understand for people who face language barriers or difficulty reading complex medical instructions.

Our solution aims to provide multilingual medicine information, visual guidance, voice assistance, and prescription-based reminders through a simple and accessible interface.

## 🚨 Problem Statement

Many people struggle to understand medicine instructions because of language differences, complex medical terminology, and limited access to clear healthcare information. This can lead to confusion about prescribed medication schedules and missed doses.

## 💡 Our Solution

MAATRA aims to bridge this gap by presenting medicine information in a simple, user-friendly format. Users can access information in their preferred language and use visual and voice-based assistance to better understand their prescribed medication schedules.

## ✨ Key Features

* 🌐 **Multilingual Support:** Access medicine information in regional languages.
* 💊 **Medicine Information:** Understand medicine-related information in a simplified format.
* 🗓️ **Medication Reminders:** Get reminders based on prescribed schedules.
* 🔊 **Voice Assistance:** Listen to medicine information where supported.
* 🖼️ **Visual Guidance:** Understand medication instructions using visual elements.
* 👨‍👩‍👧 **Caregiver Support:** Help family members assist users with medication schedules.
* ♿ **User-Friendly Interface:** Designed to make healthcare information more accessible.

*Features may vary depending on the current prototype implementation.*

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Open the MAATRA web application.
2. Select a preferred language, if available.
3. Explore the medicine information provided.
4. Use visual or voice assistance where available.
5. Follow the medication schedule prescribed by a qualified healthcare professional.
